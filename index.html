Program Flow

September 5th Gala Itinerary

Join us from morning till evening in the Main University Auditorium for an action-packed day of honor, music, games, and laughter.

09:00 - 09:30 AM

Campus Arrival & Red Carpet Teacher Welcome

Student council badge distribution, traditional tilak, and welcome flower garlands for all faculty members at the auditorium foyer.

09:30 - 10:30 AM

Inaugural Lamp Lighting & Principal's Keynote Address

Saraswati Vandana prayer, tribute to Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan, and official welcome address by dignitaries.

10:30 - 12:30 PM

Student Cultural Extravaganza (Act Phase 1)

Solo & group dances, classroom comedy skits, retro acoustic band tributes, and poetry recitals dedicated to faculty.

12:30 - 02:00 PM

Grand Festive High-Tea & Luncheon Banquet

Pass Included

Networking lunch, student-faculty interaction booths, photo booth, and dessert counters.

02:00 - 03:45 PM

Faculty vs Student Fun Olympiad & Pictionary

Hilarious dumb charades, teacher trivia showdown, musical chairs, and stand-up acts.

03:45 - 05:00 PM

Campus Awards Gala, Grand Cake Cutting & Memento Honors

Announcement of voting winners, memento presentations to each teacher, 50kg festive cake cutting, and closing DJ jam.
